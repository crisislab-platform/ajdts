Contact Us

The Australasian Journal of Disaster and Trauma Studies is an online journal published by the School of Psychology, Massey University, New Zealand under ISSN: 1174-4707.
The journal's web presence can be found at http://trauma.massey.ac.nz.

The Joint Centre for Disaster Research, which exists within the School of Psychology, in the College of Humanities and Social Sciences at Massey University, is responsible for editorial control and publishing of this journal, which has been in existence as an online journal since 1997.
